Home/FAQ
Can every program be customized?

Yes. Destinations, duration, activities, accommodation, transport, meals, and support can be tailored based on needs and operational feasibility.

Are website prices final?

No. Displayed prices are starting prices based on each package and minimum group size. Final proposals depend on dates, availability, participants, location, and program scope.

Does Conscious Travel serve companies and schools?

Yes. We provide corporate packages, study tours, CSR programs, event planning related to corporate travel, and custom journeys for communities and travelers.

Is online booking and payment available?

Not yet. Inquiries currently begin through WhatsApp or the form, followed by proposal and coordination with our team.

Do CSR programs guarantee specific impact outcomes?

Activities, documentation, and indicators are agreed in the proposal. We avoid unverified outcome claims and tailor intended targets to the program context.

How do I start an inquiry?

Share the service, group size, dates or period, destination, budget, and journey goals through WhatsApp or the inquiry form.
