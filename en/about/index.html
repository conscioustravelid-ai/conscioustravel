Conscious Travel

Our Story

Conscious Travel was founded by Merlin Ohara after years of leading and curating journeys across Europe, the UK, and Asia. Experiencing more than 20 countries and 50 cities shaped her belief that the best journeys are not only about moving between places, but about how we show up, learn, and connect.

Having lived and worked across Jakarta, Bali, Xiamen, Singapore, Madrid, and Belgium, Merlin saw the value of travel that is personal, organized, culturally immersive, and open to community contribution. Conscious Travel was created to bring this approach to corporate travel, study tours, CSR programs, and custom experiences.

Direction

Our Vision

To become a meaningful travel company that creates journeys consciously and sustainably, helping more people and organizations experience the world with deeper purpose.

How we move

Our Mission

  • Design meaningful destination management with relevant quality and budgets.
  • Encourage more responsible transport and accommodation choices where possible.
  • Create room for community contribution, cultural learning, and environmental awareness.
  • Maintain travel satisfaction through personal and organized coordination.
  • Apply risk-aware coordination and customer support before and during each program.

Meet the Founder

Merlin Ohara

Founder, Conscious Travel

Merlin is a travel curator and trip leader with experience across more than 20 countries and 50 cities. Her work leading journeys in Europe, the UK, and Asia, including as a Super Trip Leader at JoinMyTrip, shaped Conscious Travel's personal, impactful, and organized approach.
